FIRSTSHIP · A TAKE-HOME GUIDE

Follow one idea through your app.

Your idea → a small plan → a task → your own check → saved progress

1. You describe your idea

src/App.jsx collects the learner’s answers and uses the course’s local scoping rules. The learner edits the returned plan before approving it.

2. The coach proposes a smaller version

src/lib/domain.js creates a small editable plan from the learner’s idea. src/lib/guidance.js builds troubleshooting checklists. Both run in the browser; AI coding happens separately in the learner’s coding tool.

3. A confirmed check becomes progress

completeTask in src/lib/domain.js requires every check plus a reflection. src/lib/storage.js validates and saves that project in the browser. The interface shows a warning if saving fails.

A practice to reuse

Choose one observable result before asking an AI to build. Then try that exact action yourself. “The AI said done” and “I saw the result work” are different evidence.

Try it: complete one lesson check, refresh the page, and confirm that the recorded progress remains. The automated browser tests repeat this same kind of check.
