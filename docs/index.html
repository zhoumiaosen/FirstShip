A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Before you
take the first step.

Clear expectations make a better beginning.

Does FirstShip write the app for me?+

FirstShip coaches you through the process. You build your app in a separate AI coding tool, such as Codex, using prompts and checks from your learning plan. FirstShip does not control Codex or edit your project files.

Do I need coding experience?+

No. FirstShip is designed for people with little or no coding experience, and the online learning space opens in your browser. To build your own app, you will still need access to a separate coding tool such as Codex and time to try the steps yourself.

Can I really use my own idea?+

Yes. Describe your idea and who it helps. FirstShip suggests a smaller first version with one useful workflow, a definition of done, and a list of features to save for later. You can edit the plan before approving it.

Do I have to wait a day between lessons?+

No. All five days are available from the start. Five days is a suggested learning rhythm, not a calendar lock or a guarantee that every idea takes the same amount of time.

How does FirstShip guide me?+

Structured lessons turn your own idea into an editable plan, then guide you through ten tasks with project-specific prompts, expected results, and checklists. When you are stuck, an error-report exercise helps you describe the problem and take a focused prompt to Codex. AI coding happens in your separate tool; FirstShip does not inspect your code or automatically grade it.

Where is my progress saved?+

Your plan, checks, and reflections stay in the browser you use. There is no account or cross-device sync. Download a summary to keep a separate copy. FirstShip’s planning and troubleshooting guidance run in your browser without sending your course answers to an AI provider. Remove secrets and private information before sharing anything with an external coding tool or service.
